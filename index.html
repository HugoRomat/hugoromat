I am a Senior Research Engineer at Microsoft Research, in the Extended Perception, Interaction & Cognition (EPIC) group, where I build and ship large-scale, AI-driven user experiences across Copilot, OneNote, and Xbox.

My current focus is Generative UI — turning generative models into a new substrate for interfaces that adapt and respond to people in real time. As technical lead of Microsoft Research's Generative User Interface initiative, I work across diffusion-based and agentic approaches, driving cross-org collaboration with the Copilot, Windows, and M365 teams. This builds on 5+ years at the intersection of applied AI and human–AI interaction, shipping generative-AI and machine-learning features to 500K+ monthly active users. I am inventor on 9 patents and author of 15+ publications at venues such as CHI, UIST, and IEEE TVCG.
